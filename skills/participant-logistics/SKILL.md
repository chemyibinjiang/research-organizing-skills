# Participant Logistics

## Establish Identity and Scope

Start from the organizer-approved support roster, not every person in a registration
export. Use stable person IDs and reconcile aliases carefully; duplicate VIP and
session-speaker entries do not create two guests. Count unique people separately
from trips, room nights and duty shifts.

Keep necessary private data outside the skills repository. The blank
[logistics template](assets/logistics.template.csv) uses references to private
contact records instead of embedding phones or identity documents.

## Keep Arrangements Independent

- Registration: participation, fee status and approved exemptions.
- Accommodation: hotel, check-in/out, booking evidence, payer and payment status.
- Meals: eligibility, voucher prepared, voucher handed over and actual attendance.
- Transport: inbound/outbound date/time, flight/train, terminal/station, vehicle
  provider, driver, escort, passenger count and handover point.
- Event duties: speaker/chair/reviewer/interview/visit assignments and acceptance.

An accommodation column with a hotel name is not a reservation or proof of paid
room fees. Conference payment is not hotel payment. Meal entitlement is not a
delivered voucher. Confirm which dates are nights versus checkout dates.

## Make Each Handoff Actionable

Name the responsible teacher/organizer, actual escort and backup. A student escort
and a supervising teacher may be different people. Record who supplies the vehicle
and who pays separately. Never infer school transport from a general support label.

Check arrival and departure independently. For uncertain times, request the missing
detail; do not fill it from last year's flight or the guest's report time. Share
only the details the driver, hotel or escort needs, using an authorized channel.

For tours and volunteers, define each duty's start/end, meeting point, group ID,
route, explanation responsibility, handover and emergency contact. Distinguish
hotel escorts from staff waiting on campus, and room lecturers from campus guides.
Keep pairs and capacity constraints when regrouping; use registration order only
when actual registration timestamps exist. Payment timestamps are not a proxy.

## Freeze and Communicate Changes

Give the roster a cutoff/version and explicit remaining uncertainties. After a
freeze, record additions, removals and group transfers as changes, not a silent
replacement list. Recount every affected bus/group including accompanying staff.
Check actual receiving acknowledgement before declaring an assignment covered.
Any send/shared edit still needs authorization.

## Post-event Records

Separate teachers' service-credit requests from students' volunteer hours and
expense reimbursements. Use the current institutional rule supplied by the user;
do not assume all helpers receive the same score. Record dates, duties, attendance
evidence and suggested credit for human approval. Prevent double counting training
already included in a day allowance. A reimbursement promise is not reimbursement
approval or payment.

Return a deduplicated roster plus a short exceptions list: missing owner, missing
contact route, unconfirmed booking, undelivered voucher, missing departure, capacity
or role conflict. Do not describe every unknown as a failed arrangement.
