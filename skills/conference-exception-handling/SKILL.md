# Conference Exception Handling

Use this for disrupted plans, not every routine edit or unread message. Start from
the current-state index, relevant topic handoff and actual source of the change.
For a small correction, keep the response small. For a multi-person disruption,
use the [incident record](assets/incident-record.template.md) in private project
storage. Do not put real incidents or correspondence in the skills repository.

## Establish What Changed

Record the previous approved arrangement, new evidence, source/time and exact
uncertainty. Distinguish a confirmed cancellation from a rumour, an unanswered
request from a refusal, and a changed file from a changed decision. Read the
relevant attachment or full message before relying on a quoted summary.

Identify the affected deadline and who can decide. If the case is unfamiliar,
describe it in its own terms rather than forcing it into a known scenario.
Ask the smallest question that would change the next action.

## Triage and Limit the Impact

- For a reversible discrepancy with no imminent dependency, prepare a scoped
  correction and record what still needs checking.
- For an imminent session, travel or printing deadline, name a decision owner,
  backup and decision-by time with timezone. Preserve unaffected arrangements.
- For an uncertain send/payment, suspected privacy exposure or physical-safety
  concern, pause the affected operation and escalate to the responsible human.
  Do not retry, promise money, rewrite history or direct physical operations on
  an assumption. Do not wait for a complete administrative record before alerting
  the responsible on-site person to an immediate safety concern.

Urgency does not establish authority. A deadline passing does not mean a proposed
fallback was approved. If no decision-maker responds, record the blocker and
use only an explicitly pre-authorized contingency within its stated conditions.

## Trace Dependencies and Propose a Recovery

List only affected people, resources and artifacts, including indirect effects:
replacement chair duties, review eligibility, passenger counts, printed copies,
assistant-mediated contact routes, slides and who must receive the change.
Keep stable person/submission IDs and the agreed programme fields unchanged unless
the authorized change requires otherwise.

Offer feasible alternatives with their tradeoffs, prerequisites, reversibility
and latest decision time. Include doing nothing or deferring when that is viable.
Check relevant time, capacity, eligibility and ownership constraints rather than
optimizing one task at the expense of another. An alternate room or escort is not
available merely because the proposed schedule has an empty slot.

Use the available programme, reviewer, logistics or materials workflow for the
domain checks. They are helpers, not sources of missing facts. Refresh the evidence
immediately before a consequential action; reopen the decision if the facts changed.

## Approve, Apply and Notify

Confirm who approved which option and its scope. Existing explicit authorization
may cover routine implementation; new recipients, spending, duties, shared edits
or other material commitments outside that scope need approval. Permission to
update a record does not also permit sending a message.

For authorized work, make the narrow change in the current working copy, preserve
human edits and record superseded versions. One maintainer updates the master;
other topic chats should not race to overwrite it. No blanket reissue of every
file or invitation when only one group is affected.

Prepare targeted notifications with what changed, what stayed the same, effective
time/version and required action. Use the agreed contact route and minimum necessary
personal information. Track sent, acknowledged and carried out separately. If a
critical handoff is unacknowledged, escalate to its owner/backup through an authorized
channel at the agreed checkpoint; do not invent receipt or repeatedly resend.

## Verify and Close

Set closure criteria appropriate to the incident: replacement accepted the duty,
changed deck was tested, updated sheet reached the affected judges, or an uncertain
mail attempt was reconciled. "Drafted", "sent" and "file generated" alone do not
establish resolution. An approved correction is still pending until the needed
handoffs and execution are verified.

Keep unresolved items with an owner and next check. If a workaround restores the
event but leaves a follow-up, record that distinction. Close only against evidence;
an explicitly accepted residual risk needs its decision owner and rationale.
Reopen a closed item when new evidence invalidates its outcome.

Return a concise update: **change; impact; proposed/approved action; owner and
deadline; communication/execution status; unresolved checks**. Update the topic
handoff and current-state index after confirmed changes. A follow-up note is not
a scheduled reminder; create one only when requested and supported by a tool.

Read [the fictional scenarios](references/scenarios.md) for unfamiliar failure
modes or a tabletop review. This skill coordinates reasoning and handoffs; it is
not a monitoring daemon or an autonomous recovery engine.
