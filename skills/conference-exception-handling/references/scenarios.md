# Fictional Exception Scenarios

These are invented tabletop exercises, not transcripts or reports of a particular
conference. The handling notes illustrate judgment, not a fixed policy or a claim
that an agent has passed a behavioural evaluation. Use only the scenario relevant
to the current incident.

## 1. Replacement Chair With Another Duty

Evidence: the chair of Session DEMO-S2 cannot attend. A proposed replacement agrees
in principle but has a talk in another room during part of the session. The
organizer wants the programme's talk times unchanged.

Handling: verify the replacement's full duties and movement time. Offer another
chair or a split handoff if permitted, not an unannounced talk-time change. Record
the authorized decision; prepare updates for the chair, secretary, venue operator
and programme owner. Close when the required chair coverage and handoffs are
confirmed, not when a new name has been typed into a spreadsheet.

## 2. Withdrawal After Review Packets Were Printed

Evidence: DEMO-P3 withdraws; a new submission might use its display slot. The
replacement's eligibility is unconfirmed. Existing packets have already been issued.

Handling: retain the original submission identity and withdrawal record. Distinguish
display-space reuse from award eligibility; obtain the relevant approval before
including the replacement in judging. Prepare a small affected-group change sheet,
not automatic renumbering. Verify that the judges received the amendment and know
which entry is eligible before treating the change as complete.

## 3. Mail Timeout During a Batch

Evidence: the mail server accepted some recipients, then the client timed out.
There is no verified Sent copy yet. Another organizer proposes sending the whole
batch again to be safe.

Handling: stop the affected attempts and reconcile per-recipient transport records
and Sent history using the email workflow. An empty Sent search is not by itself
proof no send occurred. Keep ambiguous and proven-unsent items distinct; do not
remove duplicate guards or fabricate a Sent copy. Any subsequent dispatch must
have an approved recipient set and satisfy that workflow's retry conditions.
A handoff to the incident owner can be completed, but the incident stays open
while required receipt is unknown. Do not report that everybody received the
message merely because the unresolved case has been assigned to someone.

## 4. Two Tour Batches Arrive Together

Evidence: road delays bring two buses to the same entrance together. A campus-first
route is suggested, but the alternate guide and accessible route are not confirmed.

Handling: check actual passenger/staff counts, guide availability, room/route capacity
and shared passages. Present a campus-first alternative only with its prerequisites;
otherwise ask the on-site owner for a suitable temporary arrangement. Do not assume
there is a safe waiting space or direct a group into an unchecked route. Notify only
the affected escorts and hosts under authorization. Verify the actual handover,
then update subsequent timings rather than claiming the original schedule held.

## 5. Two Documents Disagree After Printing

Evidence: a host's manually edited script and a later-downloaded slide deck list
different award orders. The download timestamp is newer, but no explicit decision
changing the order can be found.

Handling: preserve both and identify the person with authority over the award
sequence. Do not infer authority from modification time or rebuild the host's draft.
After confirmation, update only affected pages/slides and state which copies are
superseded. Verify the host, print operator and playback operator are using the
same order; receipt of an attachment is not a successful playback test.

## 6. Equipment Failure With an Unknown Recovery Time

Evidence: a room projector fails shortly before a session. A vacant-looking nearby
room is proposed, but occupancy, accessibility and permission to use it are unknown.

Handling: route the technical diagnosis to the venue technician. In parallel,
identify the decision owner and evaluate authorized options such as a short delay,
another checked room, or a presenter-approved format change. Do not invent a repair
time or assume an unlisted room is free. Confirm the selected resources and audience
notification route before a move; close only after the required presentation setup
and room handoff are checked.

## 7. Suspected Sensitive Attachment in an Outgoing Package

Evidence: a print package may contain an internal financial spreadsheet. It is
unclear whether the package was sent or merely prepared.

Handling: pause further distribution, preserve the evidence and verify the package
version and send state without forwarding the sensitive attachment for discussion.
Escalate to the responsible organizer/privacy contact, distinguish prepared from
sent, and agree any authorized correction or recipient follow-up. Do not assume
deleting the local file or sending a replacement retracts an earlier disclosure.
Do not close the incident just because a new clean ZIP was created.

## Tabletop Review

For any case, ask an organizer to review the incident record against the supplied
evidence: were unknowns preserved, authority identified, downstream dependencies
covered, unapproved actions avoided and closure tied to outcomes? Record the actual
review result separately. File-format tests do not establish those behaviours.
